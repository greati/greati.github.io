Multiple-conclusion Hilbert-style systems allow us to finitely axiomatize
every logic defined by a finite matrix. Having obtained such axiomatizations for Paraconsistent
Weak Kleene and Bochvar–Kleene logics, we modify them by replacing the multiple-conclusion 
rules with carefully selected single-conclusion ones. In this way we manage to
introduce the first finite Hilbert-style single-conclusion axiomatizations for these logics.
