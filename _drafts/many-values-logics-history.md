---
title: Key historical aspects of many-valued logics (MVL)
---

# 1920s
- 1920: \Lukasiewicz created MVL as a separate subject, in Poland. The idea
was to model modalities, but the result was the well-known \Lukasiewicz systems
and related theorical results.
- 1921: Post introduced the idea of truth-degrees and studied them in the context
of representability of functions.

# 1930s
- 1932: G\odel tried to understand intuitionistic logic in terms of many truth
degrees.
    - G\odel systems were born.
    - Intuitionistic logic cannot be characterized by a finite logical matrix.
- 1936: Jaskowski presents an infinite-valued logical matrix for intuitionistic logic.
- 1938: Bochvar presents a philosophical application related to paradoxes.
- 1938: Kleene presents a mathematical application related to partial functions and relations.

# 1950s
- 1951: McNaughton presented an analytical characterization of the truth degree functions
definable in the infinite valued propositional \Lukasiewicz system.
- 1958/1959: Chang presented a completeness proof for the same system, but 
introducing the notion of MV-algebra as an algebraic semantics.
- 1959: Dummet presented a completeness proof for the infinite valued
propositional G\odel system.

# 1960s
- 1962: Scarpellini made clear that the first-order infinite valued \Lukasiewicz system
is not (recursively) axiomatizable.
- 1963: Hay and Belluce/Chang proved that the addition of an infinitary inference rule
leads to an axiomatization of L_\infty.
- 1965: Zadeh started an (application oriented) approach toward the formalization of vague
notions by generalized set theoretic means, which soon was related by Goguen (1968/69)
to phisolophical applications.
- 1969: Horn presented a completeness proof for first-order infinite valued G\odel logic.

# 1970s
- 1975/1979: development by Zadeh of applications of notions of vagueness 
as fuzzy sets.
- 1979: Pavelka presented an extension of MVL by a graded notion of inference
and entailment.

# 1980s
- We have fuzzy sets and their applications as a hot topic that called for theoretical
foundations by methods of many-valued logics.
- 1983: Ragaz presented the first complexity results concerning the set of logically
valid formulas in first-order infinite valued \Lukasiewicz logic.
- 1986: Mundici started a deeper study of MV-algebras.
